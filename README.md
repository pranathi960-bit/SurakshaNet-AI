# 🛡️ SurakshaNet AI

## AI-Powered Digital Safety & Scam Detection Platform

SurakshaNet AI is a digital safety platform designed to help users identify and understand online scams, fraudulent messages, fake law-enforcement notices, phishing attempts, and digital-arrest scams.

## 🚨 Problem

Online scams are becoming increasingly convincing. Fraudsters may impersonate government agencies, police officers, banks, delivery companies, and other trusted organizations.

Digital Arrest scams are especially dangerous because victims may be threatened with arrest or legal action and pressured to transfer money or share sensitive information.

## 💡 Solution

SurakshaNet AI helps users analyze suspicious content and understand potential scam indicators so they can make safer decisions online.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🔍 Suspicious content analysis
- 🚨 Digital Arrest scam awareness
- 🏛️ Fake law-enforcement impersonation detection
- 💰 Money-laundering scam awareness
- 📱 Suspicious message analysis
- 🤖 AI-assisted safety guidance
- 🇮🇳 Telugu language support
- ⚡ Fast risk assessment
- 🌐 Simple and user-friendly interface
